Privacy Policy

App: វចនានុក្រមព្រះអភិធម្ម — Abhidhamma Dictionary (package com.sovathana.abhidhamma)
Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information the app uses. In short: the app has no accounts, does not ask for your name, email or location, and keeps your searches and favorites only on your own device. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some device information as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone only. It is never sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer:

  • Your favorite words
  • Your recent search history (up to 15 searches)
  • Your settings: theme, text sizes, and the last page you read in the reference PDF

You can clear the search history inside the app, and you can remove all of this data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

2. Dictionary content and PDF reference book

The dictionary entries and the reference PDF book are included inside the app. They work without an internet connection. Opening the PDF does not send any information anywhere.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner and native ads. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, app usage, and approximate location derived from the IP address, in order to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This is done by Google, not by the developer. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalized.

  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android under Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact location varies by device).

4. Text-to-speech (listening to words)

The speaker buttons use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example, Google Text-to-Speech). The word or definition you choose is passed to that engine on your device. Depending on the engine and voice, it may process text online. The app itself does not store or send this text. Please see your speech engine provider's privacy policy for details.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – used only so that ads can be loaded.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or precise location.

6. Sharing and copying

If you use the Share or Copy buttons on a word, the text you chose is handed to the app you pick (or to the clipboard). The app does not collect or track this.

7. Children

The app is a Buddhist reference dictionary intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and your rights

The developer does not hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing to delete or export on the developer's side. Data kept on your device can be removed at any time as described in section 1. For data collected by Google AdMob, please use Google's own controls and policies linked above.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, adding analytics), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change.
